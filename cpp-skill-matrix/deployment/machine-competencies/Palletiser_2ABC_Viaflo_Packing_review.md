# Palletiser 2ABC - Viaflo Packing

Source reviewed: Tekpak Automation T-PAL-25x2 Installation, Operation & Maintenance Manual, serial 186597-01, document 186597-01_OM V1.0.

## Proposed competency coverage

The 20 competencies cover five practical assessment themes:

1. Safe isolation, guarded access, safety reset and controlled start/stop.
2. HMI navigation, recipes, alarms, manual operation and controls diagnostics.
3. Robot, pallet de-stacker, layer-card, pick-and-place, wrapper and labeller fault-finding.
4. Daily/monthly preventive maintenance and mechanical inspection.
5. Belt, chain, bearing, gearbox and sensor maintenance/adjustment.

## Assessment guidance

- 0 - No knowledge: cannot explain or perform the task safely.
- 1 - Limited: understands the task but needs close supervision and has fewer than three successful occurrences.
- 2 - Trained: has completed the task correctly on more than three occasions and follows site procedures independently.
- 3 - Able to train others competently: can diagnose variation, explain hazards and verify another person's work.

## Site validation required

- Confirm the local LOTO and guarded-access procedure before assessing safety competencies.
- Confirm which HMI settings and manual commands are authorised for maintenance personnel.
- Define the approved robot recovery boundary. The supplied manual shows robot status and fault functions but does not provide teach-pendant jogging instructions.
- Cross-check PM frequencies against Baxter's CMMS plan and operating hours.
- Obtain the referenced electrical diagrams, pneumatic diagrams, alarm list and spare-parts documentation for deeper controls and component-level competencies.
